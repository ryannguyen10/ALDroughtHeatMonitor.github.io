In a typical year every county has a 20% chance of being in its driest 20%. Values well above 20% mean the forecast leans dry; values below 20% mean it leans wet. Skill above 0 means the calibrated forecast beat climatology in leave-one-year-out testing for this county, period and season.

County boundaries: U.S. Census TIGER. Forecast: ECMWF S2S ensemble, calibrated per county against ERA5-Land with empirical quantile mapping. The S2S model grid (1.5°) is much coarser than a county, so neighbouring counties share much of the same forecast signal; county differences mostly reflect local climatology. Experimental research product, not an official drought designation.
